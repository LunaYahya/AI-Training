## Day 2 – Probability and Distributions
The second day of the week involves studying the key elements of probability and probability distributions, which serve as an essential part of machine learning. In other words, 
probability is used to quantify the level of uncertainty, whereas probability distributions describe the way values will be distributed.

## Learning Objectives
During the lab session on this topic, we have studied the basic principles of probability that include
complement, addition, multiplication,
and conditional probability. Furthermore, Bayes' theorem was introduced as a technique to revise probabilities according to the new pieces of evidence,
Moreover, we have become familiar with normal, binomial, and uniform distributions.

## Key Concepts
Why Do Probabilities Matter in Machine Learning: The predictions made by ML models are mostly expressed through probabilities, therefore, the probability theory is an important concept to consider
Probability Rules: Application of complement, addition, and multiplication rules in solving probability problems. Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem: Comprehending how probabilities change according to new evidence. 
Probability Distributions: Identifying characteristics of normal, binomial, and uniform distributions.
