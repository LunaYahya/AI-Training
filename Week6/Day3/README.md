# Day 3 — Backpropagation, Gradient Descent & Optimizers

## Learning Objectives
* Understand the four-step training loop of a Neural Network.
* Understand the role of Forward Propagation, Loss, Backpropagation, and Weight Updates.
* Understand Gradient Descent and how it minimizes the loss.
* Understand the role of the Learning Rate in model training.
* Understand Backpropagation conceptually and the role of the Chain Rule.
* Familiarize yourself with common optimizers such as SGD and Adam.
* Understand the meaning of Epochs and Batches.
* Understand how different learning rates affect the training process.

## Key Topics
* Neural Network Training Loop
* Forward Propagation
* Loss Calculation
* Backpropagation
* Gradient Descent
* Loss Surface
* Learning Rate
* Chain Rule
* Optimizers
* SGD and Adam
* Epochs and Batches

## Hands-On Lab (Tasks)
* Step 1: Draw or describe the four-step Neural Network training loop in a Markdown cell for the project notebook.
* Step 2: Experiment with a tiny Neural Network using three different learning rates (too high, too low, and good) and plot the loss curves.
* Step 3: Explain in your own words what Backpropagation computes and why the Chain Rule is involved.
* Step 4: Open a Pull Request with the current project notebook for the mid-sprint Mentor Code & Notebook Review and address the feedback.
